# Marketing-Data-Project
My first SQL project — analyzing marketing data using SELECT, JOINs, GROUP BY, and aggregate functions to derive actionable business insights on customer segmentation, campaign ROI, and sales performance.
-----
I used MySQL tool, and here is my ten questions for the project.
# Query Results & Analysis

This project answers 10 business questions using SQL:

1. How many transactions were completed during each marketing campaign?
2. Which product had the highest sales quantity?
3. What is the total revenue generated from each marketing campaign?
4. What is the top-selling product category based on total revenue?
5. Which products had a higher quantity sold compared to the average?
6. What is the average revenue generated per day during campaigns?
7. What is the percentage contribution of each product to total revenue?
8. Compare average quantity sold during vs outside campaigns
9. Compare revenue generated during vs outside campaigns
10. Rank products by their average daily quantity sold
